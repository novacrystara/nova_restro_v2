## **Points to consider for web page creation**

Let’s **not make the landing page feel like an enterprise software website**.

Nova-Restro's opportunity is to make the owner think:

> **"Holy shit. This is exactly how I want my restaurant to work."**

Not:

> "Looks like another POS with 47 features."

So visually, I'd make it **product-led**:

**Big statement → beautiful UI → short explanation → animation → next big statement.**

Very little dense text.

For example:

> **Your tables take the order.**

*Visual of customer ordering*

↓

> **Your kitchen knows instantly.**

*Visual of KDS*

↓

> **Your waiter knows when food is ready.**

*Visual notification*

↓

> **Your manager knows where attention is needed.**

*Visual restaurant floor*

↓

> **And you know how every outlet is performing.**

*Multi-outlet dashboard*

↓

# **One restaurant. One connected system.**

That would make Nova-Restro feel much more like a **modern category-defining product** rather than another restaurant POS.

And honestly, **I think the strongest positioning for Nova-Restro is not "Restaurant POS."**

It's:

# **"The operating system for your restaurant."**

The POS is merely one part of it.
